    Glossary

    AMCL
    Adaptive Monte Carlo localization; the particle-filter localizer in ROS and Nav2.
    Belief
    Posterior probability distribution over the state given all data, bel(x_t).
    C-space
    Configuration space: the space of all robot poses, with obstacles grown by the robot shape.
    Correspondence
    The assignment of an observed feature to a map landmark.
    DoF
    Degrees of freedom: independent coordinates describing the configuration.
    EKF
    Extended Kalman filter: Kalman filter applied to Taylor-linearised models.
    Holonomic
    A robot whose controllable DoF equal its DoF.
    ICR
    Instantaneous centre of rotation.
    Inverse sensor model
    p(m_i | z, x): probability of cell occupancy given a measurement.
    Kidnapped robot
    A robot moved to an unknown pose without being told.
    Likelihood field
    Measurement model scoring beam endpoints by distance to the nearest obstacle.
    Log odds
    l = ln(p / (1 - p)); turns binary Bayes updates into additions.
    Markov assumption
    Given the current state, past and future are independent.
    MCL
    Monte Carlo localization: particle filter for localization.
    MHT
    Multi-hypothesis tracking: a mixture of Gaussians, one per association history.
    Nonholonomic
    Constrained in velocities by non-integrable constraints (e.g. no sideways slip).
    Odometry
    Pose estimated by integrating wheel-encoder motion.
    Particle deprivation
    No particle lies near the true state, so the filter cannot recover.
    RRT
    Rapidly-exploring random tree: sampling-based planner.
    SLAM
    Simultaneous localization and mapping.
    Support polygon
    Convex hull of ground contact points; static stability requires the CoM projection inside it.
