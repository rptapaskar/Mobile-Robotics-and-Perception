    Mobile Robots and Perception

    By Dr. Rakesh P. Tapaskar, Center for Automation and Robotics Research (CARR Lab), KLE Technological University, Hubballi

    Complete lecture notes, classroom slides and live simulations for probabilistic mobile robotics: kinematics, path planning, Bayes filtering, motion and sensor models, localization and mapping.

    Using these notes

    As notes
    Each chapter follows the lesson schedule: one topic per class hour, with explanations, derivations, worked examples and real-life examples.
    As a presentation
    Open a chapter and press P. Each block becomes a full-screen slide sized for a projector. Arrow keys, Space or a presenter clicker move through slides; O shows an overview; F toggles full screen. Simulations keep working on the slides.
    Offline
    Fonts, equations and figures are bundled; the site works without internet once downloaded.
